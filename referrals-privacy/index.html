Wisdom Referrals Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Wisdom Surgery Clinic runs the Wisdom Referrals app and referrer portal so dental practices can refer patients to Dr Anna Raymond and those working at Wisdom Surgery Clinic. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is stored and who it is shared with. We handle personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

Who uses the app

The app is for referring dental practices and their staff. Patients do not use it. Practices give us patient details so we can accept and manage a referral.

What we collect

About your practice and your login

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your practice name, address, phone, email and provider number.
  • Your practitioners and locations, and the logo and colours you choose.

About the patient you refer

  • Name, date of birth, address, phone and email, and a parent or guardian's name where given.
  • The teeth chart, the reason for referral and your cover note.
  • X-rays and other files you attach.
  • Your signature on the referral.

Other things you send

  • Messages between your practice and our team about a referral.
  • Questions you ask Gemma, our virtual assistant.
  • A notification token and your device's name, so notifications reach the right device.

Why we collect it

We use this information only to run the referral service: to receive and assess referrals, book patients, keep your practice updated, answer your questions and send you notifications. We do not use it for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it.

What stays on your device

  • Face ID. Your face is checked by your device. The app never sees it. Your sign-in is kept in your device's secure keychain.
  • Speaking to Gemma. When you use the microphone, your device turns your speech into text. Only the text is sent. If your device cannot do this itself, Apple's speech service does it under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Your app settings, such as theme and chosen practitioner.

Where your information is stored

Referrals, files, messages and account details are stored with our database provider, Supabase, in Sydney, Australia.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. Each receives only what it needs.

  • Microsoft 365 sends emails from our practice mailbox, such as a copy of a referral you have sent. Our Microsoft 365 data is held in Australia.
  • Apple delivers notifications to your device. Notification text never includes a patient's name.
  • Google (Gemini) powers Gemma. Your question and the conversation so far are sent to Google, which may process them in the United States or other countries. Gemma answers from our app instructions only and cannot see any referral or patient record. We use Google's paid service, under which Google does not use your questions to train its models. Please do not include patient details in a question; the app blocks numbers that look like Medicare, tax file or bank details.

We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools in the app.

How long we keep it

Once a patient's treatment is complete, the referral is transferred to our practice software and deleted from our portal server 90 days later, together with its attachments and messages. Your practice's copy in the app is deleted at the same time.

You can delete your account at any time in the app's settings. This removes your login, your profile and any unsent drafts. When the last login for a practice is deleted, its practitioners and locations are removed too. Referrals already sent to us stay part of our clinical records and follow the 90-day rule above.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can update your practice details in the app's settings. To see or correct any other information we hold, contact us using the details below.

Questions and complaints

For any privacy question or complaint, contact us at hello@wisdomsurgery.clinic or on 1300 111 324.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
